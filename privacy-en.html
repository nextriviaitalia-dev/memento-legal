Memento Privacy Notice

Version 3 · 18 October 2026

Memento is an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that collects your materials in spaces and finds them again with an artificial intelligence assistant that runs on the device. This notice explains which data are processed, where, why and for how long. It is written under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Data controller

Riccardo Fatone, Via Entratico 4, 00188 Rome (RM), Italy («the Developer»).

Contact for any matter concerning personal data: nextriviaitalia@gmail.com.

No data protection officer has been appointed: the cases of Article 37 GDPR do not apply.

In short

  • Memento works on your device. It has no servers, requires no account, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Your materials, chats and everything the app produces stay on the device. The Developer does not receive them and cannot see them.
  • The artificial intelligence runs on the device: your content is not sent to external AI services.
  • The Developer receives data only when you write a report (see below).

Data kept on the device

Materials (notes, documents, photos, scans, recordings, videos, links), extracted texts and your corrections, space chats and chats with Memento, acquired knowledge (facts you tell about yourself, notes), Memento's calendar, quizzes, flashcards, concept maps, the PDFs, charts and tables you create, translations, settings and saved templates.

They are stored in the app's protected storage, encrypted by the system until the device is first unlocked after it starts. They may be included in the device backups managed by Apple (iCloud or computer) according to your settings.

Processing on the device

These happen on the device, without sending the content to third parties:

  • recognition of text, handwriting included, and of photo content (Apple's Vision framework and, if you download it, the model's vision pack);
  • transcription of recordings and videos (on-device speech recognition);
  • assistant answers, PDFs written on instruction, quizzes, flashcards and concept maps (local language model);
  • charts, totals and tables of movements (computed by the device);
  • translations (Apple's Translation framework, on device);
  • recognition of dates, search and keywords.

When something leaves the device

  • Model downloads: model files are downloaded from Hugging Face, which receives the IP address like any website. None of your content is sent.
  • Translation languages: language packs are downloaded from Apple through iOS, under Apple's terms.
  • Links you save: to read their text Memento contacts the page's website, which receives the IP address and the technical information of a normal visit.
  • Sharing and export: spaces, PDFs, CSV and Excel files and map images are sent only to whom you choose (AirDrop, Messages, Mail or other).
  • Apple Calendar: the dates you choose to add become events of your calendar and follow your iCloud sync settings.
  • Apple Watch: iPhone and Watch exchange recordings, questions and app status over Apple's direct link between the two devices. They do not go through the Developer.
  • Widgets, Siri, Shortcuts, notifications and Live Activities: they use data that stay on the device. Notifications are created by the device, without servers.
  • Purchases: the one-time purchase and the subscription are handled by Apple, an independent controller of payment data. Memento only receives confirmation that the purchase is active.
  • Reports and suggestions: when you write to the Developer, the message leaves from your own mail app to nextriviaitalia@gmail.com. The Developer receives your email address, the text you write and, if you leave it on, app version, device model, iOS version and language.

How long

  • Reports: kept as long as needed to handle them and in any case no longer than 24 months, then deleted. You can ask for earlier deletion.
  • Data on the device: as long as you keep them. You delete them whenever you want (single materials, a space, or Settings › Delete all data) or by deleting the app.

Recipients and transfers outside the EU

  • Reports arrive in a Gmail mailbox: Google is its provider and data may be processed in the United States. Google participates in the EU‑US Data Privacy Framework, recognised as adequate by the European Commission.
  • Apple (purchases, backups, calendar, translations), Hugging Face (models) and the websites of the links you save process the data they receive as independent controllers, under their own notices.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Articles 15–22 GDPR).

  • Data on the device: you can exercise them directly in the app. You can export a space in open formats, edit or delete single materials, delete a space or all data.
  • Reports you sent: write to nextriviaitalia@gmail.com. We answer within one month, as Article 12 GDPR requires.

If you believe the processing infringes the GDPR you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome — garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.

Permissions

Camera, microphone, speech recognition, photos, calendar, notifications and Face ID are requested only when you use the related feature. You can revoke them at any time in iOS Settings.

Minors

Memento is rated 12+. It does not ask for age and does not collect data at the Developer other than through reports. If you are not yet of the age your country requires to use an online service on your own (14 in Italy), send reports only with the consent of whoever holds parental responsibility.

Changes

If this notice changes, we update the version and date at the top. When the change is relevant, the app asks you to read it again before continuing.
