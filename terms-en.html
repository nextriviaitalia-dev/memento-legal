1. What Memento is

Memento is an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch to collect personal information (notes, documents, photos, scans, recordings, videos, links) in separate spaces and find it again with search and an AI assistant that runs on the device. With your materials the app can also write documents and PDFs, prepare quizzes, flashcards and concept maps, translate, draw charts and compute totals, and export tables as CSV and Excel.
